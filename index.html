About & Research Focus

I am an Assistant Professor-Teaching (Limited Term) in the Department of Communication and Media Studies at York University. My research critically examines media governance, telecommunications policy, legal frameworks, and digital rights, with a primary focus on internet shutdowns, platform regulation, and digital connectivity across the Global South.
